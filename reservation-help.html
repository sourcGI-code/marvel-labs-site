How do I reserve?

Select your trailer size and Buy or Lease plan, review the reservation and current policies, then continue to Shopify. Enter contact and payment details there. Shopify confirms the order after successful payment.

Will checkout charge me?

Yes. This is a live checkout for a one-time $100 USD refundable reservation deposit. Applicable tax, if any, is calculated at checkout. Review Shopify’s final total before paying. Any Shop Pay installment option applies to this deposit checkout; it is not the $0 monthly trailer lease or financing for the full trailer price. It is not the full trailer price or a lease payment, and no remaining balance is charged automatically.

How do I change or cancel my reservation?

Contact jaxon@marvellabs.com with your Shopify order number. Cancellations are refunded in full to the original payment method under the current refund policy.

Where is my confirmation?

Shopify handles order confirmation. Returning to this website does not confirm or cancel payment. If you are unsure whether an order completed, check your Shopify confirmation and contact reservation support before trying again.

What are the final lease terms?

The Lease plan has a $0 monthly lease fee. The $100 refundable reservation deposit is separate. The operator receives 25% of profit from products sold. Operating costs, duration, maintenance, insurance and return obligations are confirmed in the final written agreement. See the plans and earnings page.

When will my trailer arrive?

Target delivery: Spring 2027. Timing is not guaranteed and is confirmed separately before proceeding.
