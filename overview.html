Understand the business model

See what was made.
Know what you actually earned.

Under the Buy plan, the operator keeps 75% of product profit and Marvel receives 25%. The Lease plan has a $0 monthly lease fee and no upfront trailer purchase; the operator keeps 25% of profit from products sold and Marvel receives 75%. The $100 refundable reservation deposit is separate. Operating costs, duration, maintenance, insurance and return obligations are confirmed in the final written agreement.

Those percentages apply to profit from products sold, not the selling price. The app is intended to show products produced, products sold, calculated operator earnings and actual payouts separately. Your payout follows the operating costs and profit calculation in your agreement.
