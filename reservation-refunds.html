How live refunds would work

The proposed live policy allows a full refund of the $100 reservation deposit. Private support details and refund instructions will be provided before paid reservations open. For the current demo, see reservation help.

Refunds would be returned through the original payment method. Provider processing and bank posting times vary. A separate final purchase or lease agreement would explain terms for the completed trailer transaction.

If you proceed with the trailer

The deposit is credited toward your trailer’s final price. It is not a separate non-refundable booking fee. The final invoice or agreement would show the credit.

What this preview does

No funds are collected, held or refunded. Demo references are examples and do not identify actual paid orders. All policy wording here is draft material for reviewing the launch experience.
