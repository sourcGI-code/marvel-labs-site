CURRENT PREVIEW PRIVACY NOTICE

Privacy Policy

Your information. Kept simple.

Updated: November 4, 2026

The reservation demo

The current reservation flow is a local demonstration. Name, email, phone, company and service-area entries stay in the page’s memory. The reservation demo does not submit them to Marvel Labs, Stripe or any other service. They are cleared when the page reloads or the tab closes. Use the supplied example details to try it.

What this tab remembers

The demo uses session storage for the selected trailer size, plan, demo payment-method label, demo reference, status, timestamp and agreement version. It does not store contact details or card information. This allows the same tab to show the demo confirmation after a refresh. The record is normally removed when the tab’s browser session ends.

Confirmation email preview

The confirmation message is generated in your browser and is not sent. If you download the email draft, the file includes the recipient email you entered and your demo reservation details. It is saved on your device. Opening or sending that draft in your own mail app is your choice and is governed by your mail provider’s practices.

Payments

No payment processor is connected to this preview. No card number, bank details or wallet credentials are requested, stored or charged. Payment-method choices and the cancellation/refund state are simulations.

Website feedback

The feedback link opens GitHub’s issue form. Nothing is submitted automatically. If you submit an issue, its contents are public and GitHub’s privacy practices apply. Use it for website feedback, without personal or payment details.

Hosting and external services

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub and its delivery infrastructure may process technical request data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the website. Fonts and visual assets are served with the site or its linked media hosting. The reservation demo does not add advertising trackers or analytics.

External links, including the separate Marvel Crew app, lead to services or experiences with their own data handling. A future live payment integration will require an updated privacy notice describing the processor and the information shared.

Questions about this preview

See reservation help. Private support details will be provided before paid reservations open. This policy describes the current preview, not a future live checkout.
