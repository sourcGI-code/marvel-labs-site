05 / FAQ

STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Questions,
answered.

What is the Marvel Labs microfactory trailer?

An equipment trailer for landscaping crews with a 6 ft enclosed factory at the front. Mowers ride in the rear cargo bay. The factory turns bagged grass clippings into boxed sinks. It is a concept with a Spring 2027 delivery target.

Who is it for?

Landscaping crews and lawn-care operators who already mow and bag clippings on their routes.

How does it work?

Tip bagged clippings into the hopper. The enclosed factory makes and boxes a sink while you work. The boxed sink comes out of the side hatch, you drop it at The UPS Store on your route, and the Marvel Crew app confirms the hand-off.

What sizes are there?

Two sizes with the same 6 ft enclosed factory. The 16 ft is 7 ft wide with a 10 ft rear cargo bay. The 20 ft is 8.5 ft wide with a 14 ft rear cargo bay.

How much does it cost?

Preview Buy pricing is $44,999 for the 16 ft and $49,999.99 for the 20 ft, with a 75% operator profit share. Lease comes with a 25% operator profit share; lease terms are set in a separate agreement. Taxes, delivery and registration are not included.

What is the profit share?

The operator's share of profit on sinks sold, not a share of the selling price. The site's example of 20 sinks at $15 each ($300 a day) is an illustration, not a projection. Earnings are not guaranteed.

Is the $100 deposit refundable?

Yes. The $100 reservation deposit is fully refundable with no cancellation fee, and it is credited toward the trailer's final price. It is not a monthly lease payment. The reservation on this site is currently a demo and takes no payment.

When will trailers be delivered?

Spring 2027 is the delivery target. It is not a guaranteed date, and availability is limited. Final availability, service area and delivery are confirmed before the trailer agreement.
