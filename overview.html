Understand the business model

See what was made.
Know what you actually earned.

Under the proposed Buy plan, the operator keeps 75% of product profit and Marvel receives 25%. Under the proposed Lease plan, there is no upfront trailer purchase; the operator keeps 25% of product profit and Marvel receives 75%.

Those percentages apply to profit, not the selling price. The app is intended to show products produced, products sold, calculated operator earnings and actual payouts separately. Final operating costs and the written profit calculation matter; illustrative earnings are not guaranteed returns.
