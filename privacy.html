The reservation form

The reservation form prepares a draft in your email app. Its fields are processed in your browser. This site does not store or submit your entries. You decide whether to send the email.

If you send an email to Marvel Labs, the company receives the information you include and can use it to respond to your inquiry. Your email provider’s own terms and privacy practices apply.

Analytics and hosting

This site does not include advertising trackers, Google Analytics, or Microsoft Clarity. Fonts and visual assets are served with the site. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical request data, such as IP addresses, to deliver and secure it.

External links

Links to LinkedIn and other external sites lead to sites with their own privacy practices.
