What still needs measurement.

The trailer is a product under development. Design renderings and workflow animations explain the intended system; they do not establish measured production performance.

Published today.

  • Design illustrations for 16 ft and 20 ft trailers, with nominal factory and rear cargo layout lengths.
  • A proposed process for converting suitable collected grass into a 20 × 20 inch sink.
  • Preview Buy pricing and proposed operator shares of profit from products sold.
  • An interactive Marvel Crew app demonstration using example data.

Evidence still to publish.

  • A dated physical production demonstration with input condition, cycle time and accepted output.
  • Product test results, reject rate, operating hours and downtime under stated conditions.
  • Power source, energy consumption, charging requirements and measured runtime.
  • Validated feedstock acceptance limits and preparation requirements.
  • Verified trailer weights, usable clearances, towing ratings and representative loadouts.

No measured trailer throughput, validated operating-cost result or guaranteed return is claimed by these pages. Missing values are listed as pending.

How to read the visuals.

The homepage films and model images are design and workflow illustrations. App counts, locations, camera views and earnings are example data. The $300 earnings example assumes 20 completed sales at an illustrative $15 gross operator earnings per sale, before operator expenses; it is not a test result or a projection for either plan.

Target delivery: Spring 2027. Timing is not guaranteed and is confirmed separately before proceeding.
