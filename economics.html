Operator earnings = the agreed profit on products sold × the operator’s plan share. This is not a share of the selling price and is not earned simply by producing an item.

The final agreement must define deductible costs, accepted sales, rejected or returned products, unsold inventory, shipping, refunds, settlement timing and payout timing. Those definitions have not yet been published as final terms; a net earnings forecast would be premature.

The $300 example.

20 sales × $15 = $300

Illustration only: all 20 sinks sell at $15 gross operator earnings each, before the operator’s expenses. It is not a production test, a forecast, or a projection for either plan. The $15 amount is an illustrative input, not a calculated payout under the unfinished profit agreement.

Actual earnings depend on sales, costs and the agreed profit calculation. Production, demand and earnings are not guaranteed.

Responsibilities to confirm.

Marvel’s proposed package combines factory inputs, packaging, shipping arrangements, buyer coordination and software. The operator brings their own landscaping equipment. The final agreement must assign consumables, power, maintenance, insurance, storage, shipping, damage, returns and downtime costs.

Target delivery: Spring 2027. Timing is not guaranteed and is confirmed separately before proceeding.
