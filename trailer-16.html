Before reserving.

Can my truck tow it?

Tow-vehicle compatibility cannot be confirmed yet. Empty and loaded weight, GVWR, tongue weight, brakes and hitch requirements are pending. Confirm the complete loaded configuration and vehicle ratings before towing.

Does $100 buy or lease the trailer?

No. It is a one-time refundable reservation deposit. The 16 ft preview Buy price is $44,999; final purchase and lease terms are separate. The Lease plan has a $0 monthly lease fee. Operating costs and other lease obligations are separate and confirmed in the final written agreement.

Are the films production demonstrations?

The website films are design and workflow illustrations. They are not measurements of output, quality, operating cost or unattended runtime. See the development-status page for what has and has not been published.

How do I cancel a reservation?

Email jaxon@marvellabs.com with your Shopify order number. See the current refund policy.

Who supplies and maintains the system?

Marvel’s proposed package includes the trailer factory, production inputs, packaging, shipping arrangements, buyer coordination and software. Consumables, maintenance responsibilities, warranty and downtime procedures require the final operating agreement.
