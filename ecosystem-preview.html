A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Clear from the start.

Do I need to replace my landscaping equipment?+

No. Crews bring their existing truck, mowers, trimmers, and tools. Marvel supplies the trailer and the proposed factory and platform services. Equipment fit must be checked against the final trailer specification.

Do crews have to find buyers for the products?+

Marvel handles the product buyer relationships and coordinates orders. The intended crew workflow is to do landscaping work, load suitable collected material, retrieve packages, and drop them at UPS.

How is the crew’s share calculated?+

Marvel supplies the production ingredients, packaging, and shipping. Agreed costs are deducted from product sales before the applicable operator profit share is calculated. Final costs, acceptance conditions, fees, and payout timing are set in the written agreement.

What can I try on this website today?+

You can explore the trailer models, watch illustrated workflows, use the example earnings calculation, try the app prototype, and walk through the reservation demonstration. Production performance and the complete connected service are still being developed.
