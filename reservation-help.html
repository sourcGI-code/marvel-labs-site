Reservation help

Before your next move.

Version: November 1, 2026

How do I try the whole experience?

Choose a trailer and plan, continue to Details, and select Use demo details. Review the summary, choose a simulated payment method, read the linked agreement and check the acknowledgment. Reserve for $100 then opens the demo confirmation.

Will this charge me?

No. Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay are simulated, and no real payment information is collected. A DEMO reference is not a paid order.

Can I change my selection?

Use Back or Edit trailer before confirming. After confirmation, Manage reservation lets you preview changing the configuration or cancelling for a full $100 refund. No real purchase, cancellation or refund is made.

What do Buy and Lease mean?

Buy is shown at $44,999 for the 16 ft or $49,999.99 for the 20 ft, with a 75% operator profit share. Lease is shown with a 25% operator profit share. The $100 is the reservation deposit, not a monthly lease payment. Final Lease and profit-share terms require a separate agreement.

Is the deposit refundable?

The proposed reservation deposit is fully refundable and credited toward your trailer’s final price if you proceed. Try the cancellation flow from Manage reservation. See the refund policy preview.

When would I get a trailer?

Spring 2027 is the target in this preview. Final availability, specifications and delivery arrangements are confirmed separately. Completing the demo does not reserve a production slot.

Where is my confirmation email?

Choose View confirmation email after completing the demo. The message includes your reservation number, trailer, plan and simulated $100 deposit. You can download it as an email draft. Email delivery is being connected; the confirmation screen shows its status. Once enabled, using your own email address sends a real message clearly marked DEMO. Example details only create a preview. If sending fails, you can retry without repeating the demo payment. After cancelling, the same button previews a cancellation email. Save confirmation opens a printable copy. All confirmations are clearly marked as demos.
