Website analytics choices

We use optional Microsoft Clarity analytics on marvellabs.shop to understand which sections visitors reach, which links they use, and where they leave the reservation process. Tracking starts only after you choose “Allow analytics.” Declining leaves these analytics off.

When allowed, Clarity collects page visits, clicks, scrolling, device and browser information, and a visual reconstruction of your interaction with the website. It uses cookies to connect visits across pages. Microsoft processes the data to provide Clarity and improve its products. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement and Clarity data collection documentation.

Form entries and embedded app/chat content are masked. Our custom analytics events contain predefined section, scene, trailer-size and plan labels; we do not send names, email addresses, chat messages, payment details or customer identifiers in those events. Checkout and payment take place on Shopify. A checkout handoff on this website does not establish that a deposit was paid.

Use “Privacy choices” in the website footer to change your decision. We remember it in this browser for up to 180 days. Withdrawing consent reloads the current page to stop the recorder. We also keep these analytics off when your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track. Localhost previews do not send analytics.

These choices cover optional website analytics. The Marvel Labs privacy policy covers our wider services and checkout. Contact hello@marvellabs.com with questions.
