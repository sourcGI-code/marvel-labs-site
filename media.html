The trailer story.
The facts behind it.

Marvel Labs develops autonomous microfactories that combine manufacturing equipment, software and production processes. Its trailer concept is designed for landscaping crews. It pairs room for their equipment with a system meant to turn suitable grass clippings into products.

marvellabs.shop is Marvel Labs’ official site for trailer details and reservations. The wider corporate platform is at marvellabs.com.

Current offer at a glance.

Two design sizes

Nominal sizes: 16 ft × 8.5 ft and 20 ft × 8.5 ft. Both are 7.5 ft high. Only the length changes. Final usable clearances, towing ratings and payload are still pending.

Reservations

You pay a one-time, refundable $100 USD deposit through Shopify. It is separate from the trailer price and lease cost. It does not authorize an automatic charge for the balance.

Reservation help and current policies

Buy or Lease

Buy for $44,999 (16 ft) or $49,999 (20 ft), or choose the $0 lease. The operator’s share of profit on products sold is 75% with Buy and 25% with Lease, under the agreed profit calculation. Operating costs and obligations still apply.

Keep illustrations separate from evidence.

The films are design animations. App screens show example data. Development focuses on suitable grass clippings. Limits on accepted clippings, measured output rate, runtime and reliability are not published yet.

The $300-per-day figure is an illustration. It assumes 20 products sell at $15 gross operator earnings each, before operator expenses. It is not measured output, a forecast or a return for either plan. If you use the number, include these assumptions.
