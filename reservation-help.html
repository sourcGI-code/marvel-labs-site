Reservation help

Reserving a trailer.
Your questions, answered.

How do I reserve?

Pick a size and a plan (Buy or Lease), review your reservation and our current policies, then continue to Shopify. You enter your contact and payment details there, and Shopify confirms the order once payment goes through.

Will checkout charge me?

Yes. Checkout is live and charges a one-time, refundable $100 USD reservation deposit, plus any applicable tax calculated at checkout. Check Shopify’s final total before you pay. A Shop Pay installment option, if you use one, applies only to this deposit: it is not the $0 trailer lease and not financing for the full trailer price. The deposit is not the trailer price or a lease payment, and no remaining balance is charged automatically.

How do I change or cancel my reservation?

Email hello@marvellabs.com with your Shopify order number. Cancellations are refunded in full to your original payment method under the current refund policy.

Where is my confirmation?

Shopify sends your order confirmation. Coming back to this website doesn’t confirm or cancel a payment. If you’re not sure an order went through, look for your Shopify confirmation and email us before trying again.

What are the final lease terms?

The lease is $0, and the $100 refundable reservation deposit is separate. On a lease you receive 25% of the profit from products sold. Operating costs, lease length, maintenance, insurance and return terms are set in the final written agreement. See Specs & pricing on the home page.

When will my trailer arrive?

Reservations are for Spring 2027. We confirm your delivery date with your order.
