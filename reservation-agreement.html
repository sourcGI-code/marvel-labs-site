01 / What you are reserving

The proposed reservation records your preferred trailer size and Buy or Lease plan with Marvel Labs Inc. It is a reservation, not the final purchase or lease agreement. You would review and accept final specifications, pricing and terms before proceeding with the trailer.

02 / Your $100 deposit

The proposed deposit is fully refundable. If you proceed, it is credited toward your trailer’s final price. It is not an additional fee. A reservation would become active only after successful payment; an unfinished checkout or an email request would not create a paid reservation.

This demonstration does not collect the deposit. Its confirmation number begins with DEMO and does not hold a production slot.

03 / Cancellation and refunds

You can preview cancellation from Manage reservation. The proposed live policy is a full refund of the $100 reservation deposit, without a cancellation fee. Live refunds would be returned through the original payment method. No real money moves in this preview.

Private support details and refund instructions will be provided before paid reservations open. Processing time would depend on the payment provider and the customer’s bank. See the refund policy preview.

04 / Configuration and pricing

The 16 ft platform is shown at 7 ft wide, with a 6 ft 2½ in enclosed factory and 9 ft 9½ in rear cargo bay. The 20 ft platform is shown at 8.5 ft wide, with the same factory and 13 ft 9½ in rear cargo bay. Renderings show the design concept; final usable clearances, equipment compatibility and specifications require confirmation.

Preview Buy pricing is $44,999 for the 16 ft and $49,999 for the 20 ft. Taxes, delivery, registration and any requested options would be shown in the final agreement. The $100 deposit is not the full cost of a trailer and does not establish a monthly Lease price.

05 / Buy and Lease plans

The displayed profit shares are 75% for Buy and 25% for Lease. They refer to the operator’s share of profit from sinks sold, not a share of the selling price. The profit calculation, eligible costs, quality standards, settlement timing and operating responsibilities must be set out in a separate written agreement.

Lease duration, ownership, ongoing charges, maintenance, insurance and return conditions are not established by this reservation preview. Production, product sales and earnings are not guaranteed.

06 / Production and delivery

Spring 2027 is the current target shown in this preview, not a guaranteed delivery date. Final availability, your service area and the delivery arrangement would be confirmed before the trailer agreement. No final balance payment is authorized by reserving.

07 / Before the final agreement

The live purchase or lease documents will need to specify the final configuration, total price, delivery, cancellation rights, warranty, service arrangements, safe operation, insurance, ownership and the profit-share calculation.
