The trailer story.
The facts behind it.

Marvel Labs develops autonomous microfactories, combining manufacturing equipment, software and production processes. Its trailer concept is designed for landscaping crews, pairing equipment space with a system intended to turn suitable collected grass into products.

marvellabs.shop is Marvel Labs’ official trailer product and reservation site. The broader corporate platform is at marvellabs.com.

Current offer at a glance.

Two design footprints

16 ft × 8.5 ft and 20 ft × 8.5 ft nominal trailers. Both are 7.5 ft high; only the length differs. Final usable clearances, towing ratings and payload are pending.

Reservations

A one-time $100 USD refundable deposit is paid through Shopify. It is separate from the trailer price and monthly lease fee, and does not authorize an automatic balance charge.

Reservation help and current policies

Buy or Lease

Buy prices: $44,999 for 16 ft and $49,999 for 20 ft. Lease: $0 monthly lease fee. Operator profit shares are 75% for Buy and 25% for Lease, on products sold under the agreed profit calculation. Operating costs and obligations still apply.
